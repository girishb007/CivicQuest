# Contributing to CivicQuest

Thank you for helping shape CivicQuest. At this stage, the most valuable contributions may be product questions, user research, design critique, civic-domain knowledge, safety review, and documentation, as well as code.

## Before you start

1. Read [README.md](README.md) and [CivicQuest_Project_Vision.md](CivicQuest_Project_Vision.md).
2. Check existing issues and pull requests.
3. Open an issue before starting a substantial feature, architecture change, or policy change.
4. Do not include real personal data, identifiable people, or private evidence in issues, designs, fixtures, or pull requests.

## Ways to contribute

- Clarify or challenge the product vision.
- Improve the Mumbai civic taxonomy or accountability model.
- Review the Figma flows for usability and accessibility.
- Identify privacy, moderation, legal, or abuse risks.
- Improve documentation.
- Add implementation work once the technical direction is agreed.

## Pull requests

Pull requests should:

- Explain the problem and the proposed change.
- Link to a relevant issue where one exists.
- Keep the scope focused.
- Include screenshots or design links for UI changes.
- Include tests or explain why tests are not applicable.
- Call out privacy, safety, moderation, or data-model implications.
- Avoid exposing personal information or unverified allegations.

Use a clear title such as `docs: clarify guest onboarding` or `feat: add report status model`.

## Discussion standards

Be respectful, specific, and open to revision. Disagreement about product direction is welcome; harassment, doxxing, targeted accusations, and attempts to identify private individuals are not.

## Design and product changes

When proposing a new feature, describe:

- The user problem.
- Who benefits and who could be harmed.
- The smallest useful version.
- Trust, safety, privacy, and moderation considerations.
- How success could be measured.
