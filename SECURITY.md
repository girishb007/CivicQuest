# Security, Privacy, and Abuse Reporting

CivicQuest is a civic reporting concept that may eventually process location data, images, accounts, and reports about people or public places. Please treat privacy and abuse concerns as high priority.

## Do not publish sensitive details

Do not open a public issue for:

- Security vulnerabilities that could affect users or infrastructure.
- Private personal information or identifying evidence.
- Unverified accusations about an identifiable person.
- Content involving minors or vulnerable people.
- Credentials, tokens, secrets, or private links.

For now, contact the repository owner privately through the GitHub profile: [@girishb007](https://github.com/girishb007). Include a concise description, affected area, reproduction steps where safe, and the potential impact. Do not send live personal data unless it is essential.

## Product safety boundary

The current concept is not authorization to expose, punish, or identify alleged defaulters. Before any public launch, the project needs documented decisions for consent, evidence handling, identity and face visibility, moderation, appeals, retention, jurisdiction, lawful escalation, and protection against false or malicious reports.

Contributors should use synthetic or consented examples only.

## Disclosure

Please allow maintainers reasonable time to understand and address a report before public disclosure. We will acknowledge reports when possible and coordinate next steps with the reporter.
