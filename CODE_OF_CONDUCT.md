# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CivicQuest is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role in the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Welcome different perspectives and lived experiences.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, evidence, and implementation.
- Protect privacy and do not share personal information.
- Report safety, abuse, and moderation concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, targeted humiliation, malicious accusations, sharing identifiable private evidence, and any other conduct that would make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate. Safety and privacy reports can be sent privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing CivicQuest in public spaces.
